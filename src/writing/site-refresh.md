I've rebuilt the site from the ground up. The old version was running on a setup I hadn't
touched in years, which was a big part of why nothing new ever went up here.

The mission hasn't changed: research roundups, occasional writeups, and tools for teachers —
especially teachers working with students with disabilities and English Language Learners. The
[Behavior Referral Tracker](https://johnboniello.com/Behavior-Tracker) and
[Research Roundup](https://johnboniello.com/Research-round-up/) are both still around and linked
from the homepage.

I have also started to build some little apps, mostly for use in my home with my daughter or wife. I thought it would be good to release them for anyone to use. 

As I work on new write-ups they will be posted on this site.
