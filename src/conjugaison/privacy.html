Privacy Policy, Conjugaison FR

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy is short, because the app does not collect anything.

Conjugaison FR runs entirely on your device. It does not request internet access, it does not use the camera, it does not create accounts, and it does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information at all. Your practice progress stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third party services of any kind built into this app.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects nothing, there is nothing that could identify or track a child who uses it.

Changes to this policy

If that ever changes, I will update this page and the date above.
